Privacy

Payload HTTP does not create an account and does not send your saved requests, tokens, or passwords to a server run by the developer.

Requests you send go only to the address you enter. Tokens and passwords are stored in the iOS keychain on your device. Saved requests stay in the app’s local database. A one-time purchase is processed by Apple.

The app does not sell personal information and does not use advertising or tracking.
